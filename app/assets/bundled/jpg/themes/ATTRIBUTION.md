# Theme background images

Background images of the built-in image themes (`crates/mnemos_core/src/ui/theme/mnemos_themes.rs`).
They are embedded in the app binary (`Assets` in `app/src/lib.rs`) and never fetched. The same
credits are in the repository's `NOTICE`.

| File | Theme | Title | Author | Source | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `mountain_night.jpg` | Mountain Night | Night Sky (Grand Teton National Park) | NPS Photo / Bill Bjornstad | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Night_Sky_(55545879347).jpg | Public domain, US federal work (NPS) |

| `fjord.jpg` | Fjord | Geiranger fjord, vertical cliffs-2 | Szilas (Wikimedia Commons user) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Geiranger_fjord,_vertical_cliffs-2.jpg | Public domain, released by the author (PD-self) |
| `stone.jpg` | Stone | Stone (sandstone with layered ridges) | TwinCurrent ApS, generated procedurally | Original work, no third-party image. Source: `script/generate_stone_image.py` | AGPL-3.0-only, like the rest of Mnemos IDE |

Each photo was cropped and tone-mapped for Mnemos: cropped to 16:10, resized to 2560x1600,
regraded, and masked so the area behind the text stays calm. The National Park Service and the
photographers do not endorse Mnemos.

`stone.jpg` is generated by `script/generate_stone_image.py`, which is its source; regenerate it
with `python3 script/generate_stone_image.py stone.png` and
`magick stone.png -strip -quality 86 app/assets/bundled/jpg/themes/stone.jpg` (the script's
docstring lists the parameters to retune it).

Replacing an image: keep it 2560 px wide, at most 700 KB, with EXIF and GPS metadata stripped, and
run `cargo test -p mnemos_core --lib theme` and `cargo test -p mnemos --features gui --lib themes`.
The second checks that the theme's text, accent and ANSI colours stay readable over the image,
in the text area and in the bottom band where the input editor sits.
