Microsoft Visual C++ runtime

vcruntime140.dll, vcruntime140_1.dll and msvcp140.dll (Microsoft Visual C++
Redistributable 14.44, Copyright (C) Microsoft Corporation) are copied into the
Windows installer from app/assets/windows/<arch>/. They are Microsoft binaries,
not covered by the AGPL, redistributed under the "Distributable Code" terms of
the Microsoft Visual Studio license
(https://visualstudio.microsoft.com/license-terms/; the redistributable files
are listed at
https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/redistributing-visual-cpp-files).

They are the runtime library of the compiler Mnemos is built with, so under
AGPL-3.0 section 1 they are System Libraries: they are not part of Mnemos's
Corresponding Source, and their terms add no restriction to Mnemos's own
license.
